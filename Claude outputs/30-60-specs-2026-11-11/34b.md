## 3. Where the run fits, and travel
The facts that decide it: chapter 33 binds Nijel's Point and chapter 35 starts there (or at Ironforge if 34 is run); 96773 needs level 34 and starts at Lan'dalock in the Dalaran crater (Eastern Kingdoms), so it is only in the log after chapter 34; 3636 needs level 39 and starts in Stormwind.
- **From Nijel's Point between 33 and 35 (as numbered):** fly Nijel's Point -> Ratchet (Nijel's - Theramore - Ratchet are direct links; Ratchet is a plain flight point, 26:30, [TAR16227 Bragok] 63.1,37.2 The Barrens), ride ~10-15 min south past Camp Taurajo (chapter 26's road) to [TAR12866 Myriam Moonsinger] 49.0,94.9 The Barrens, run, ride back, fly back to Nijel's Point (or hearth). About 50-60 min of travel on top of the run. Run before 34, it has **no 96773** (not yet taken) and **no 3636** (level 38, no Stormwind visit): 7,985 XP of the 16,385 available. Run after 34, he is in Ironforge with 96773 and 35 goes to Thelsamar - RFD would mean Ironforge -> Stormwind -> boat to Auberdine -> fly Ratchet -> ride -> run -> back by Theramore -> Menethil boat -> Ironforge -> Thelsamar: about 75-90 min of travel.
- **After 35/36:** same Eastern Kingdoms problem as above; no cheaper.
- **Recommended: chapter 39's Thousand Needles leg.** The plan's step 4 already goes to Thousand Needles by Theramore / the Barrens road for the Ravaging Tempest event; Razorfen Downs sits at the south end of that same road (Barrens 49,95, by the Thousand Needles border), so the extra travel is near zero. At ~40: 3636 can be taken in Stormwind on the Ironforge stop (req 39); 96773 is in the log from 34; quest levels 35 / 37 / 41 / 42 still pay full XP at 40 (Classic rule: full XP up to 5 levels below; unverified on Forever); the mobs (34-41) are easier; and Deathchill Armor (mail) is wearable on the spot. Cost: the run (~60 min) only. The 96773 hand-in goes back to Lan'dalock (Alterac 15.9,73.1): chapter 39 visits Alterac for Bath'rah (80.5,66.9), about 65 map units east of the crater, so either a crater detour from Southshore (~15-20 min) or carry 96773 into round 2's first Southshore pass.
- **Decision for the main session / Braedyn:** place 34b on chapter 39's leg (renumber, e.g. 39b, `[NX]` back into 39), or keep it after 34 from Nijel's Point and accept losing 3636 (and 96773 if run before 34).
