## 4. Route

### Leg 0 - level check (first line of the chapter)
`[OC]` |cffffffffLevel check|r - this chapter wants **level 32** (Dutiful Steps and Ground to Keep require 32; the rest require 30). You arrive at about 31.9: if you are still 31, do Northfold Manor first (its Syndicate are level 30-32) and you will be 32 before Stromgarde. The one hard fight is the **Forsaken Courier: she and her four Forsaken Bodyguards are all level 35 and walk together**, so you pull five mobs three or four levels above you - they hit harder, you miss and get resisted more, and you cannot pull her alone. That is why the route leaves her for last, when you are 32-33: pull from range on open ground, Earthbind Totem down, Frost Shock the runners, kill the guards first only if she is not fleeing; if anyone else is on the road, invite them (launch-crowd rule). If it goes badly, let her walk on and try her next pass - her loop runs from Go'Shek Farm to Thoradin's Wall and back.
