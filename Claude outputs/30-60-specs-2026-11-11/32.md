## 3. Travel and hearth
### In: Booty Bay -> Stormwind -> Menethil -> Theramore boat (chosen)
Why this way: 1248 is in the log and its chain only continues at Mikhail in Menethil (1248 -> 1249 -> 1250 -> 1264 -> Dustwallow). Skipping Menethil loses 1248/1249/1250/1264 (3,525) and locks 1265/1266/1324/1267 (7,425), plus 1301 (690), 1260 (710) and 1132 (1,200) = **13,550 XP**. Every flight out of Booty Bay goes through Stormwind anyway (Booty Bay links: Stormwind, Sentinel Hill, Darkshire).
- Booty Bay flight master [TAR2859 Gyll] 27.5,77.8 Stranglethorn Vale -> Stormwind (TaxiPath 41, node 19 -> 2, 630c).
- Stormwind (Mage Quarter, the Blue Recluse area): Connor Rivers 51.1,95.5 and Angus Stern 51.8,93.6 Stormwind City (2 units apart). Stormwind flight master [TAR352 Dungar Longdrink] 71.3,72.2 Stormwind City.
- Stormwind -> Menethil Harbor: multi-hop via Ironforge (TaxiPath 13 SW->IF 50c, 18 IF->Menethil 330c). The game routes it.
- Total flight money about 10s 10c (630 + 50 + 330 copper; whether a multi-hop charges the plain sum is unverified).
- Menethil flight master [TAR1571 Shellei Brondir] 9.5,59.7 Wetlands. All Menethil pickups are within 2 units of 10.5,60.3.
- Boat to Theramore: Questie's transport marker "Portal To Theramore" (object 716525) at **5.6,63.2 Wetlands**; Maiden's Virtue Crewman (npc 2099) stands at 5.57,64.08 beside it. Use the pier sign wording like 22:82 ("the sign on the pier names the destination").
