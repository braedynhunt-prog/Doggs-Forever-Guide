# Troll Warlock leveling plan (OctoWoW)

Source of truth for class changes: https://octowow.st/warlock (site sits behind a DDoS check; open it in a real browser). Captured 2 Sep 2026.

## OctoWoW warlock changes that matter for leveling
- New spells: Dark Harvest (lvl 40, ranks 40/50/60, 30s CD; channeled heavy Shadow dmg, Affliction DoTs tick faster during the channel, CD resets if the target dies mid-channel; it is the Affliction capstone talent). Mana Funnel (lvl 40, rank 2 at 50). Demon Gate (lvl 60, 30 min CD, 3-min Felguard).
- Soul bags give every shard spent a chance not to be consumed (5% Small Soul Pouch → 25% Core Felcloth Bag).
- Soul Fire learned at 38 (4 ranks), 260 mana, 30s CD, scales with spell dmg. Hellfire 160 mana. Siphon Life transfers over the full duration. Conflagrate only eats 3s of Immolate. Searing Pain no longer high threat.
- Affliction tree: Improved Drains (row 2, 2 ranks, merges Imp Drain Soul/Life/Mana); Resilient Shadows (row 2, 3); Fel Concentration now 2 ranks and covers all Aff spells; Improved CoW 2 ranks; Curse of Exhaustion moved to row 3 (Improved 2 ranks); Soul Siphon (row 4, 3 ranks: Drain Soul/Dark Harvest/Death Coil dmg per Aff effect, up to 4); Malediction (row 5, 1: CoA stacks with other curses, and CoR/Shadow/Elements auto-apply CoA); Rapid Deterioration (row 5, 2: Aff cast speed, haste also speeds DoT ticks — Troll Berserking synergy); Nightfall also procs from Dark Harvest and Drains; Dark Harvest replaces Dark Pact. Amplify Curse removed.
- Demonology tree: Soul Entrapment (row 1, 3: +all dmg with no demon out); Sinister Pursuit (row 1, 2: pet speed); Demonic Aegis (row 2, 3: better Demon Armor); Soul Funnel (row 2, 2); Fel Intellect 3 ranks; Demonic Sacrifice moved to row 3 (Imp = +all spell dmg, cancelled by summoning/enslaving); Nether Studies (row 4, 3: replaces Imp Imp/VW/Succ); Improved Stones (row 4, 2); Unholy Power row 4, 3 ranks, all demons; Demonic Precision (row 5, 3: pet gets your hit/crit); Power Overwhelming (row 5, 1); Master Demonologist row 6 (Imp = cheaper spells); Unleashed Potential (row 6, 3: pet scales with your Fire/Shadow SP); Soul Link capstone works with any demon.
- Destruction tree: Shadow Vulnerability (row 1, 5: replaces Imp Shadow Bolt, procs from Shadow Bolt and Drain Soul); Demonic Swiftness (row 2, merges Imp Firebolt/Lash); Aftermath 3 ranks (+Immolate periodic, proc slows 50%); Intensity row 3 all Fire; Pyroclasm row 4 (Conflagrate can stun); Improved Soul Fire (row 5, 2); Improved Immolate = total dmg; Bane also Searing Pain; Destructive Reach also Hellfire radius.

## Pets
- Imp 1-10 (aggressive, Firebolt). Voidwalker from the lvl 10 class quest (Orgrimmar, Cleft of Shadow) through 40+. Succubus (20 quest) = more DPS, squishier. Felhunter (30 quest) for caster mobs.

## Rotation with Voidwalker
Send VW (Torment) → Curse of Agony → Corruption → (Immolate if mana is fine) → wand → Drain Life if hurt → Drain Soul to finish when shards are needed. Life Tap between pulls, VW Consume Shadows out of combat. Multi-pull: DoT everything, VW holds one, Drain Life the second, Fear only when no adds nearby. From 40: Dark Harvest as the finisher (CD resets on kill). From 44: open with Curse of Shadow (Malediction gives free CoA).

## Spells worth buying / skipping
Buy every rank: Corruption, Curse of Agony, Shadow Bolt, Drain Life, Life Tap, Demon Skin/Armor, Healthstone, Soulstone (18), Curse of Shadow (44), Death Coil (42), Dark Harvest (40/50/60). One rank is enough: Fear, Drain Soul, Health Funnel, Howl of Terror (40). Skip or buy late: Curse of Weakness, Curse of Recklessness, Curse of Tongues, Firestone/Spellstone, Shadow Ward, Sense Demons. Get Ritual of Summoning, Unending Breath, Eye of Kilrogg once.

## Gear / misc
- Wand ASAP (Lesser Magic Wand lvl 5, Greater Magic Wand lvl 13 from enchanters/AH) — it's the mana-free filler.
- Stats: Stamina + Intellect early, +Shadow Spell Damage from ~30 on (Corruption, CoA, Siphon Life, Drain Life, Dark Harvest all scale). Spirit is low value (Life Tap).
- Buy a soul bag early (shard refund chance on Octo). Tailoring + Enchanting is the classic warlock pairing (Soul Pouch, wands).
- Troll racials: Berserking (haste; use with DoTs up once Rapid Deterioration is in), Regeneration (10% more HP regen, part active in combat), Beast Slaying (+5% vs beasts — most of the Barrens/STV).
- Addons already installed that help: Cursive (DoT timers/auto-curse), Necrosis, TourGuide-Turtle + pfQuest for the class quests.
