# Professions for the warlock (OctoWoW) — researched 3 Sep 2026

## Decision so far
- Levelling: Tailoring + Skinning (skinning pays for the level 40 mount; tailoring for bags / Soul Pouch). Enchanting deferred.
- Mount rules (Turtle system, Octo runs the same): any mount usable from level 1 at +half-your-level % speed; Apprentice Riding at 40 = 90g (81g at Honored with your race), racial mount 10g. Troll racial mount = raptor (Sen'jin Village); wolves need Orgrimmar Exalted. Free Turtle mount via Darkmoon Faire quest "Torta's Egg" at 18.
- Wands: no vendor in Durotar. Only capital wand vendors (Katis in Orgrimmar, cheapest Smoldering Wand at lvl 13). Lesser Magic Wand (lvl 5) / Greater Magic Wand (lvl 13) are enchanter-made — AH.

## Endgame value
- Tailoring (keep): Bloodvine set (ZG patterns) 3-piece bonus requires 300 Tailoring; Robe of the Void; Felcloth/Mooncloth; Core Felcloth Bag 28-slot soul bag = 25% shard-refund on Octo.
- Enchanting: convenience + gold (self-enchants, DE raid greens). No exclusive gear worth noting.
- Engineering: Sapper Charges / Dense Dynamite as raid damage buttons; gold sink.
- Alchemy+Herbalism: gold + flasks.

## Jewelcrafting (Octo custom, from Turtle 1.17.2) — checked against octowow.st/db
- Trainers Horde: Orgrimmar, Undercity, Sparkwater Port. Master trainer Fanzy Sparkspring, Steamwheedle Port. Needs Mining for gems (Gemstone Deposits need Mining 310; Gemstone Clusters recycle mats). Some mats need Enchanters (Enchanted Gemstone Oil) and Engineers (Precision Jewelers Kit 175 Eng, Jewelry Scope 225 Eng).
- Specialisation at skill 225 + level 40 after a questline (quest available from 34 at the UC trainer): Goldsmith (gear, epics exclusive) or Gemology (ring/neck "gemstone" enchants).
- Gemology gems are BoP but are applied through the trade window like enchants — anyone can get them from a Gemologist. Caster-relevant: Arcane Emerald +5 spell damage (JC 295), Ancient Dwarven +3 spell power, Stunning Imperial +3 all stats, Graceful Agate +6 spell penetration, Resurged Topaz +2 mp5, Radiant Ember +6 fire dmg. No shadow-damage gem (Dreary Opal is shadow RESIST). Ring/neck must be ilvl 25+.
- Goldsmith crafts, caster-relevant (all lvl 60 unless noted):
  - Encrusted Gemstone Ring — BoP, Requires Goldsmith to wear: +8 Sta, +5 Arcane Res, 1% spell crit, +20 spell dmg. The one true personal reward.
  - Mana Binding Signet — BoP: 6 mp5, 2% on cast restore 75-85 mana (lvl 44).
  - BoE (buyable from any Goldsmith): Shimmering Diamond Band (+16 Int, +5 Sta, +18 sp), Ruby Ring of Ruin (+9 Int, +7 Spi, +13 sp, 1% spell crit), Mastercrafted Diamond Crown (cloth head: +24 Int, +8 Sta, 1% spell hit, +24 sp, 7 mp5), Mastercrafted Diamond Bangles (cloth wrist: +6 Int, +6 Sta, +16 sp, 6 mp5), Empowered Domination Rod (off-hand: +11 Int, +8 Sta, +20 sp, lvl 58), Crown of Molten Ascension (fire helm +24 sp +11 fire).
  - Tank/melee BoP: Talisman of Hinderance, Blackwing Signet of Command, Blazefury Circlet, Golden Runed Ring.
- Verdict: Mining+JC(Goldsmith) is a strong gold profession that also gives one personal epic-quality ring; but it costs the Tailoring slot. For raw warlock power Tailoring (Bloodvine 3-set + soul bag) still wins; the JC BoE pieces and Gemology enchants can simply be bought from other players.
