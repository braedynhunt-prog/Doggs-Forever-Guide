# Lessons (WoW Forever Atlas)

Format: one or two lines per entry - what went wrong, why, the rule going forward. Merge duplicates.

- 29 Oct 2026: a plan predicted the Wowhead XP data would check as fresh without comparing its pull dates (17-18 Oct) to the latest client build (28 Oct). Rule: check the actual dates before stating an expected result in a plan.
- 29 Oct 2026: Wowhead XP was called "browser-only" after testing only Wowhead's own endpoints; third-party mirrors (PandaQuest's Wowhead overrides and MCP server) were never searched. Rule: before declaring a source unreachable or manual, search GitHub and Reddit for mirrors and APIs.
- 29 Oct 2026: a subagent brief said to reuse score_items.py's weights without checking docs/forever-enh-stat-weights.md, which marks the mace term obsolete since 17 Oct. Rule: check the current doc before telling an agent to reuse constants from code.
- 1 Nov 2026: the 29 Oct loot report said two dungeons had "no creatures" and bosses' drops were "invisible" when only the zone-spawn field was empty; the NPC rows and drops were in the DB. Rule: when one DB field is empty, check the other tables keyed on the same id before declaring data absent.
- 1 Nov 2026: the 29 Oct plan said the guide "names no head item", taken from a report column produced by a text matcher (Gear:/Loot: lines only). Rule: grep the chapter before stating a gap derived from a matcher.
- 1 Nov 2026: three browser-pulled zone TSVs were piped into ingest_zone_tsv.py with cat; the first had no trailing newline, so the second zone's header landed mid-line and 62 Redridge quests were merged into Westfall (caught by the diff, restored from git). Rule: ingest one block per run, or add a newline between files, and diff row counts per zone before committing.
- 1 Nov 2026: reported "Forever campfires grant rested XP" from one Wowhead news line; the source is the Leatherworking Camp Tent, which the guide's own camping notes already say. Rule: grep the guide and docs for a mechanic before calling it new.
- 1 Nov 2026: a quest-reward scan used only QuestieDB's item questRewards field, which is incomplete for Forever items (the Spidersilk Sack had no quest link). Rule: scan rewards from the Wowhead zone JSON itemchoices/itemrewards fields, and use QuestieDB only as a cross-check.
- 1 Nov 2026: a chapter brief told the agent to pick up The Reports (291) in Kharanos without checking its prerequisites; the qdb row needs 287 and 98323 first, so the agent had to drop it. Rule: before a quest goes into a brief or overlay spec, read its preQuestGroup/preQuestSingle (quest row [11]/[12]) and its start NPC's spawn.
- 1 Nov 2026: the Phase 2 prep table flagged two Horde Zephras quests as usable because it checked only the race and class masks, not the Wowhead "side" field, and 2^33 (the Horde Skyborne bit) is not 2^32. Rule: a faction filter on Wowhead quest rows uses side (1 Alliance, 2 Horde, 3 both) first, and the race mask bit for Skyborne is 2^32 Alliance / 2^33 Horde.
- 1 Nov 2026: the overlay anchor set (quest/NPC/coordinate/title tokens) could not reach untagged [OC] notes, so all three Phase 3 agents stalled on ~100 lines until a literal-text anchor was added. Rule: when designing an edit language over a text file, include a plain-text anchor from the start and test it on the lines the report flags, not only on tagged steps.
- 1 Nov 2026: the warrior check_guide baseline came out empty twice because the grep pattern was typed from memory ("optional step without") instead of copied from the existing baseline ("optional quest step without"). Rule: when filtering tool output for a baseline, copy the match text from the existing baseline file, and check the line count before using it.
- 1 Nov 2026: the warrior build record said the Muren Stormpike chain was dropped for a "race mask without Skyborne", but the DB mask carries the Skyborne bit; the reason was never checked against the field. Rule: when a build record gives a data reason for dropping a quest, quote the field value next to it.
- 1 Nov 2026: a chapter line said the Forever DB had no Dead-tooth Jack NPC; it does (6093, "Dead-Tooth Jack") and the name search was case-sensitive. Rule: search DB names case-insensitively before declaring an entity absent.
- 1 Nov 2026: Feathers for Binding was proposed as optional without reading the quests that list it as a prerequisite (it gates 3,155 XP of the Nightfang Den chain; check_guide caught it after the edit). Rule: before proposing a quest as optional or dropped, list every quest whose preQuestGroup/preQuestSingle names it.
- 1 Nov 2026: the README told players to install into `_classic_era_`; the Forever client has its own `_forever_` folder (the beta used `_classic_beta_`). Rule: Forever addons and guides go in `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\`; the Era folder is a different client.
- 1 Nov 2026: four quoted-XP "fixes" were proposed from a regex matcher that credited every number on a line to the [QA] quest; three numbers belonged to the [QT] quests or to untagged chain quests and were right, and the fourth fix omitted a quest the chapter does (caught by the subagent before editing). Rule: before proposing a quoted-XP change, attribute each number to a specific quest id on the line (QT first, then QA, then the chain the chapter actually completes) and show that attribution in the proposal.
- 2 Nov 2026: the druid plan called Feral Charge and Primal Bite "trained at 20 / 25" from a SkillLineAbility + SpellLevels join; both are talents (Blizzard deep dive, Wowhead tooltips) and SpellLevels lists a level for talent spells too. Rule: a learn level from SpellLevels says nothing about how a spell is learned; check the acquire method / trainer list before calling a spell trained.
- 2 Nov 2026: the Phase 2 druid brief told three agents the bear band order was Str > Agi > Sta, typed from memory; the feral weights table (Sta 1.5, Agi 1.2) says Str > Sta > Agi, and the 00s chapter had to be corrected after review. Rule: a stat order or weight quoted in a brief is copied from the weights doc or the profile file, never typed.
- 2 Nov 2026: the druid overlay spec said the hearth was at Sentinel Hill from chapter 05 on, from memory of the route; chapter 06 binds Lakeshire and the Alliance guide itself hearthed to Sentinel Hill afterwards without rebinding (now a check_guide check). Rule: state hearth/bind facts from the [S]/[H] tags, and let the tooling check them.
- 2 Nov 2026: the spec said "the whole Alonso remark goes" for a shield quest and an agent also deleted the Dragonslayer's Lance quest (2,550 XP) beside it. Rule: a delete instruction in a brief names the exact quest ids and lines it covers.
- 2 Nov 2026: `git add docs/` swept two untracked files Braedyn had made (docs/forever-shaman-leveling-sheet.html/.pdf) into the research-pass commit; the rule says ask before committing changes you did not make. Rule: stage by explicit file path (the files in the diff you reviewed), never by directory, and check `git status --short` for untracked files under that directory first.
- 3 Nov 2026: a subagent brief called the Repurposed Hair Band a "cloth head" item from the slot-less name in an earlier report; the DB and tooltip say cloth wrist, and the agent caught it before scoring. Rule: an item's slot, armour type or stats quoted in a brief come from the tooltip cache or qdb item row, never from the name.
- 3 Nov 2026: the sheet review found 09:62 still quoting the Strong Fishing Pole at +45 after the 2 Nov fix corrected only 02:74; the fix went to the line reported, not to every line with the value. Rule: when a quoted value is corrected, grep every chapter (and the docs/sheet) for the old value before closing the fix.
- 3 Nov 2026: weights/score_items.py still reads the 24 Sep item_tooltips.json, so scores_2026-11-03.txt carries stale stats (Rockslicer +10 Str vs tooltip +7 Str). Rule: a scores run quoted in a guide line is made from the dated tooltips file named in the run header.
- 3 Nov 2026: two days of guide edits (druid build, Mental Dexterity rescore, sheet-review fixes) were committed and pushed but never released; the only release was 1 Nov and still said `_classic_era_`. Rule: a round ends with a GitHub release tagged with the TOC version (CLAUDE.md working agreements); compare `gh release list` to the TOC versions at the start of a guide session. Also: the warrior TOC stayed at 2026.11.02 after a 3 Nov rebuild changed a chapter line; when a variant is rebuilt with a content change, bump its variant.json Version and Notes.
- 4 Nov 2026: a research subagent was briefed to find shaman training costs from Classic sources (wiki.gg, Wowhead Classic, classicdb) and came back with nothing, while the Wowhead Forever NPC page (wowhead.com/forever/npc=258098) carries Forever's own `trainingcost` per spell and was read in two browser calls. Rule: for a Forever-specific number, read the Forever Wowhead page in the browser first; Classic sources are the fallback, and a brief should not forbid the browser.
- 4 Nov 2026: trainer and vendor steps were written without `[T]`/`[V]` (and loot/use steps without `[QC]`/`[CI]`), so Guidelime completed them on arrival within 6 yards (CurrentGuide.lua updateStepCompletion: a step whose only completable element is the `[G]` completes on the goto). Rule: every step that expects an action at an NPC or object carries the tag that holds it (`[T]`, `[V]`, `[QC]`, `[CI]`, `[SK]`); only travel steps are goto-only. check_guide.py enforces it from 4 Nov.
- 4 Nov 2026: a `[CI]` tag for the Shrine Bauble was written into the druid overlay with a guessed item id (5185) and corrected to 15877 only after a qdb lookup that should have come first. Rule: every id typed into a guide or overlay line is looked up in tooling/qdb in the same step, never typed from memory.
- 4 Nov 2026: the leveling sheet called Rockbiter 'known' at level 1 from Classic habit; SkillLineAbility AcquireMethod is 0 (trainer) for 8017, and only 331/403 are auto-learned (AcquireMethod 2) for shamans, so Braedyn could not find it in-game. Rule: a spell is called known-at-creation only when its SkillLineAbility AcquireMethod is 2; everything else is a trainer line naming the spell at the first [T] stop.
- 5 Nov 2026: 02:53 told the shaman that Two-Handed Maces is baseline "per the client"; SkillRaceClassInfo row 702 (skill 160, shaman) has Availability 0, the same as 2H Axes (row 701), so Braedyn could not equip the Coldridge Hammer. Rule: a weapon skill is known at creation only when its SkillRaceClassInfo row for that class and race has Availability 1; quote the row id and field in the line.
- 6 Nov 2026: QuestieDB 1.0.11 was pulled on 5 Nov but the guide's open claim "no Pattern: Totemic Leather Armor exists in the data" (2 Nov) was not re-checked against it; the new DB carries the pattern (252774, a bind-on-pickup Oggleflint drop), found only when the Leatherworking switch was planned. Rule: after a DB refresh, re-run every test-list claim of the form "X does not exist in the data" before closing the refresh.
- 6 Nov 2026: 02:59 sent the level-13 shaman to Fimble Finespindle for Leatherworking recipes; in game Fimble refuses to talk below level 20 (Classic: Gretta is the low-rank trainer, Fimble the Expert one), and Wowhead Forever's teaches lists show no trainer level gate - the Classic trainer split was never checked. Rule: when a city has several trainers of one profession, send the player to the one whose Wowhead list stops at the Apprentice recipes (the low-level trainer) and mark the rank trainer's level gate; a Wowhead teaches list says nothing about who will talk to you.
- 8 Nov 2026: the weights doc had held Spirit at 1.0 "until the character sheet settles it" since 24 Sep, and Braedyn had been playing since 4 Nov; the six character-sheet readings were only asked for when he questioned the weights, and three of them overturned the model (regen 2.5x the slope, base crit 0.46 not 1.7, Earth Shock raised on 3 Nov). Rule: at the first session after launch, and after every client build, walk the doc's "verify in game" list with him before any gear or weights work.
- 8 Nov 2026: build_variant.py aborted for both variants - twelve overlay anchors per variant had stopped matching after the 6 Nov rounds (Leatherworking switch, crate model), which shipped three shaman releases without rebuilding the variants; nobody noticed until the next rebuild. Rule: after every shaman chapter edit run build_variant.py for both variants (even when they are not being released) so a broken anchor surfaces in the round that broke it; a release that attaches old variant zips says so in the body.
- 8 Nov 2026: verify.py --update-baseline was run with two new findings showing and swallowed one that was a real defect (a reworded trainer line verify could not parse), only caught because the baseline diff was read afterwards. Rule: before --update-baseline, list the new findings and name the one(s) being accepted in the audit doc; if there is more than that, fix first.
- 11 Nov 2026: the README ("level 20 needs 159.1k") and the 20-30 build doc ("20-30 needs 281k") used the post-patch-2.3 XP table from memory; Classic/Forever uses the pre-2.3 table (warcraft.wiki.gg Experience_to_level: 167,200 to 20, 331,800 for 20-30, 655,400 for 30-40, 4,084,700 to 60; wowforeverleveling agrees from the game files). Rule: XP-to-level numbers come from the pre-2.3 table in that wiki page, never from memory; the two older figures are known-wrong and stay unedited in their docs.
- 11 Nov 2026: fetch_tooltips.py wrote weights/tooltips/2026-11-11.json with only the 373 items fetched that day, and verify.py reads only the newest dated file, so 21 vendor-price checks of the 1-30 guide lost their tooltips (seen when verify --all ran on a repo mirror). Rule: after a fetch_tooltips run, back-fill the previous file's ids into the new one (or make the script seed from the newest file) before running verify or committing the cache.
- 11 Nov 2026: the level-40 weights brief quoted Rage of the Storm as "35.63 dps, +15 Sta, +12 Int (tooltip 11 Nov)" from tooling/item_tips.json, a 28 Oct pull; the live tooltip reads 3.60 speed, 35.56 dps, +16 Str +15 Sta and the item was never in weights/tooltips/2026-11-11.json although the route plan says "cached". The same brief said the Resto points "do nothing in the model" without reading the talents (Tidal Focus gives +1% hit per rank, Mindfulness regen while casting). Rule: a stat line in a brief is re-read from the live tooltip the day it is written and names the file it came from; a talent is called model-neutral only after its Forever text has been read.
- 11 Nov 2026 (Phase 3 specs): the route plan's XP table ran ahead of its own text - route_xp.py's chapter lists held quests the text called out, dungeon quests and two quests 1-30 already does (chapter 27: 34,600 planned, 17,300 real; about 67k too high through chapter 37), so the predicted levels sat 2 below the quests' required levels (Faldir's Cove needs 35 at a real 33.5). Rule: the XP table is computed from the same plain list the plan text prints, 1-30 is grepped for every id first, and each chapter's highest required level is compared with the predicted entry level before the plan goes to Braedyn.
- 11 Nov 2026: the route plan listed quests as plain without reading their targets (Otto and Falconcrest, Colonel Kurzen, Bhag'thera, Tethis, the Stromgarde intruders are elite-ranked; Nagaz and Baron Vardus are level 40 for a level-32 chapter) and placed pickups without the quest's own gates (Nat Pagle 6609/6607 need Fishing 225, Alliance Trauma First Aid 225, 96773 and 97130 level 34, the Bloodsail chain level 37). Rule: before a quest is called plain in a plan, read npc row [3]-[5] (levels, rank) of every kill target and quest row [3] and [17] (required level, required skill) at the chapter where it is picked up.
- 11 Nov 2026: the route plan counted only side-1 rows of the Riverglades zone page; 33 Powderfuse Port quests (83,900 XP) have a blank side there and were dropped without a word (their quest pages say Both). Rule: a blank Wowhead side is unknown, not excluded - count those rows separately in the plan and settle them from the quest page.
- 11 Nov 2026: the Phase 3 brief expected the NPC rows to fill the unlinked Riverglades starters; they filled 17 of 77. Rule: when a brief names a shortcut around a browser pull, test the shortcut on the whole list before planning around it.
- 11 Nov 2026 (round B): a chapter writer ran tooling/tip.py for two item stats and the script rewrote the tracked cache tooling/item_tips.json (restored from git at the end). Rule: a writer brief names the scripts that write tracked files (tip.py, fetch_tooltips.py, gear_calls.py, dungeon_loot.py) as off limits and points at the dated tooltips file instead.
- 11 Nov 2026 (round B): the first totem step was written with a quest-objective index ([QC79358,1]) although QuestieDB lists the four totems in another order than the quest text; a wrong index would have held the Dustwallow step on another zone's totems. Rule: a step that collects one of several items of a quest is held with [CI item,count], not [QC id,index], unless the objective order has been read in game.
- 11 Nov 2026 (round B): chapter 29's spec assumed Hints of a New Plague 659 was done, but chapters 23 and 25 mark it [O]; check_guide caught the missing prerequisite when the chapter was written. Rule: when a later chapter builds on an earlier quest, read that quest's line: if it is [O] or conditional, the later chapter carries its own optional pickup or says what to do without it.
- 12 Nov 2026: closing the first tab of the Claude-in-Chrome tab group while a zone batch was still fetching in a later tab dissolved the whole group; the batch finished and its file was complete, but the in-page length and hash could no longer be read (checked by structure instead). Rule: close browser tabs only after the last batch of a pull is downloaded and verified.
- 13 Nov 2026: the 40-50 route plan went to Braedyn with three quests placed before their prerequisites: To The Hinterlands (1449) a chapter ahead of In Search of The Temple (1448), Zul'Farrak ahead of the Hinterlands chain that gives Nekrum's Medallion (2988 > 2989 > 2990 > 2991), and Forbidden Knowledge (737) kept although its only Alliance prerequisite is an elite quest that is out (the Horde twin was not in the pool, so the chain check skipped it). The chapter split checked that prerequisites existed, not where they sat. Rule: `python tooling/route_xp.py <route file>` now lists every required quest whose prerequisite is not done or required in the same or an earlier chapter; a plan goes to Braedyn only with that list empty or explained, and a prerequisite that is neither in the pool nor done counts as unmeetable.
