# 30-60 guide, round 1 (levels 31-42): build record - 11 Nov 2026

Round 1 of the 30-60 guide was built on 11 Nov 2026 from the route plan (`docs/30-60-plan-2026-11-11.md`) and the Phase 3 brief (`docs/30-60-build-plan-2026-11-11.md`). Eighteen chapters, files `27_...lua` to `41_...lua` in `guide/Guidelime_ForeverShaman/`, under a second Guidelime group "Forever Shaman 30-60". TOC version 2026.11.11, title "Forever Shaman 1-60". Shaman only; the warrior and druid variants are not touched.

The working papers are in `Claude outputs/30-60-specs-2026-11-11/`: one spec per chapter (route in play order, gear calls, what is out and why), `DECISIONS.md` (Braedyn's rulings), `TITLES.md` (chapter table, carried quests, training stops), `00-spec-list.md` (the list he approved), the Wowhead pull results, and the two briefs the subagents worked from.

## Rulings that shaped it (Braedyn, 11 Nov; full text in DECISIONS.md)
- Dungeons are required chapters; only their grind/luck quests are optional (RULES.md rule 2).
- Elites outside dungeons are always GROUP and optional, kept only for a scored upgrade. This removed, among others, Overlord Kilgar's chain, Komuk, Blademaster Kaijo, Snargl, Chogg'Zac, Old Rot-Chum, An Ambassador of Evil and The Lost Tablets of Will.
- Field Marshal's Log (1.6% drop-start) is a conditional line; A Fur Trade is optional with a 17-kill budget and opens Friz Frazzlespark's Powderfuse chain; Hail the Dragonslayer is out.
- Uldaman and Razorfen Downs are run in the level-40 block; the block starts when Riverglades I is done, whatever his level, in the order Arathi / ritual, Razorfen Downs, Alterac, Ironforge at 40, Uldaman.
- Chapter 37 ends with a Booty Bay hop (Bloodsail chain, Tokal). Faldir's Cove is always done in the level-40 block. The level-40 Alterac targets (Dark Council, Baron's Demise, the Parchment) are done in 39c.
- Windfury on the two-hander at 40. Powderfuse Port is chapter 41. The route plan's hearth sequence stands: Southshore (27), Booty Bay (31), Theramore (32), Nijel's Point (33), Thelsamar (35), Farholde Keep (38).
- The 1-30 chapters were not touched, except the Elemental Aid XP (2,650 -> 3,300 at 25:68, 26:16, 26:85) and one pointer line at the end of 26.
