**How to read the drop rates.** Wowhead's count/outof counts every sampled kill, including players who were not on the quest and could not see the item, so the figure is below the true on-quest rate; boss heads (only quest-holders kill them) read 90-100% and prove the sampling itself is sound. Classic Wowhead has the same bias for the same items, so the numbers are a consistent yardstick, and Braedyn's thresholds (60/30) were set looking at them. **Class-quest items are the exception**: the Twisted Totems are sampled against every player's kills of those mobs and read 4-15%, which says nothing about the shaman's rate. They are gates, so they are plain steps with an in-game budget check.

## Rules added for 30-60 (go into RULES.md at the round-1 release)
- **Optional-step test.** An `[O]` quest or step stays only for a scored gear upgrade (score_items on the dated tooltips), a bag, a food-thread recipe, or a gate (class quest, trainer, profession rank, mount, reputation the route needs). XP-only optionals are out; elite and group quests get the same test.
- **Drop-quest rule** (extends rule 3). Kill-count quests are preferred. A mob-drop collection quest is a plain step only when its Wowhead Forever rate is 60% or better, or the item also comes from a ground object, or the item is a single named mob's drop at 60%+. At 30-60% it is `[O]` and the kill line carries "drop quest, ~N kills" (N = count / rate) - **unless the route's kill quests on the same mobs already cover that budget, when it is plain with the budget label** (the Kurzen remedies, below). Under 30% it is out, silently (rule 4), and every quest behind it in a chain goes with it. A named mob's drop between 30% and 60% gets rule 3's "one kill only, then abandon" note. Class-quest items ignore the rate (see above).
