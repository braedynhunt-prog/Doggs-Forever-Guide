## Rules Braedyn set (27 Oct)
1. Quest through every new Forever zone/hub the Alliance route can reach at 1-30.
2. Cooking + fishing levelling built into the route, timed so he has the skill/level for a location's recipes before arriving; best food at every level. He only cares about two foods — **Longjaw Mud Snapper (+10 AP, 5+)** and **Mithril Head Trout (+20 AP, 25+)** — everything else is skill-up filler. Not a speed-levelling guide: 10 minutes of fishing for +10 AP is a good trade, and he'll level both professions for endgame anyway.
3. No rare-drop farm quests ("don't want to be grinding mobs for rare drops for nothing").

## Q2 — Cooking/fishing: there was no levelling plan; now there is (see Applied)
- **Forever food facts (Wowhead Forever tooltips, verified by the main session):** 10 s eat → Well Fed 15 min = stat buff **+5% XP from kills**, identical on every food. Stat tiers +1 / +3 (lvl 5) / +5 (lvl 15) / +10 (lvl 25). No buff at all from Charred Wolf Meat, Boiled Clams, Clam Chowder, Dig Rat Stew, Savory Deviate Delight. Longjaw Mud Snapper = +10 AP, level 5, Recipe Cooking 50 (Khara Deepwater, Loch Modan 40.3,39.3; also Tansy Puddlefizz IF 48.2,6.5). Mithril Head Trout = +20 AP, level 25, Recipe Cooking 175 (Stuart Fleming Menethil 8.0,58.4; Lindea Rabonne Southshore 50.6,61.0; Heldan Galesong Auberdine; Tansy IF). Succulent Pork Ribs +5 Str at 15, Cooking 110, Kendor Kabonka SW Old Town 77.5,52.7 — **Kendor also sells every quest recipe on the route** (Blood Sausage, Liver Pie, Crocolisk Steak, Goulash, Gumbo, Omelet, Wolf Kabob, Beer Basted). Expert Cookbook: Cooking 125 to read, Shandrina Ashenvale 49.5,67.1 (also Wulan, Desolace).
- **Forever fishing facts:** **Strong Fishing Pole = +45 Fishing** (Classic was +5), req Fishing 10 / level 5, sold by Khara Deepwater, Vernon Hale, Stuart Fleming, Lindea Rabonne, Tansy, Catherine Leland. Shiny Bauble +25 (no req), Nightcrawlers +50 (req Fishing 50). Big Iron Fishing Pole +42, req Fishing 100. Expert Fishing book: Old Man Heming, Booty Bay (req level 20) — off route, 30-40 guide. Zone minimums are Classic values (Dun Morogh 1, Loch Modan ~25, Redridge/Westfall ~55, Wetlands/Hillsbrad ~130): with the Strong Pole + a lure he can fish Wetlands water for trout from base ~40.
- **Gates:** Journeyman Cooking = skill 50 + level 10 (Classic value; any cooking trainer); Apprentice caps at 75, so the Crocolisk Steak reward (80, ch04) needs Journeyman by the ch03 Ironforge visit. Journeyman Fishing = 50 / level 10 (Grimnur Stonebrand IF 48.1,6.9, Paxton Ganter, Arnold Leland SW, Matthew Hooper Lakeshire, Harold Riggs Menethil). Expert Cooking = 125. Skill targets written into 01a: Cooking 50 by 12, 80 by 16, 125 by 24, 175 by ~30; Fishing 25 by 12, 50 by 15, 100 by 22, 130+ by 28.
