# Forever Alliance guide — standing rules (read before editing `Guidelime_ForeverShaman`)

These are Braedyn's rules for the guide, collected 27 Oct 2026 from the README, the build notes, the audits and the conversations that set them. Dates mark when a rule was set. If a rule here conflicts with an older note elsewhere, this file wins. Braedyn: correct anything that is wrong or missing.

## What the guide is
- A Guidelime addon (`C:\Users\Braedyn\Documents\WoW Forever Atlas\guide\Guidelime_ForeverShaman\`, zip beside it) for **Braedyn's Dwarf Shaman (2H Enhancement) on WoW Forever, Alliance**, levels 1 to about 48-49 since 13 Nov 2026: chapters `00a`-`26` are the Guidelime group "Forever Shaman 1-30" (to about 31), chapters `27`-`57` the group "Forever Shaman 30-60" (round 1: `27`-`41`, 31-42, build record `docs/30-40-build-2026-11-11.md`; round 2: `42`-`57`, 42 to about 48 on required quests, build record `docs/40-50-build-2026-11-13.md`). Round 3 (to 60) goes into the same second group and starts from the open ends the round-2 record lists. From chapter 42 on the chapters are ordered by the level their quests require, and a route plan is checked with `python tooling/route_xp.py` (prerequisites, hand-in zones, required levels) before it goes to Braedyn. The level-40 errands are a block of five chapters, `39` to `39e`; there is no chapter 36.
- **Efficient, not a speedrun** (27 Oct). A 10-minute detour that pays back over the next levels (a lasting food buff, a profession gate) is worth it. XP per hour still matters: no quest goes in without knowing its XP ("how do we know what quests are worth it otherwise?", 17 Oct).
- **Human and Hunter lines are no longer maintained** (27 Oct, brother not playing). They stay in the files, hidden by `[GA Human]` / `[A Hunter]`; don't spend effort on them, don't delete them without asking.
- Project docs hold notes only; code, data and the addon live on his PC under `WoW Forever Atlas\` (24 Sep).

## Content rules — what goes in
1. **Every new-to-Forever Alliance zone, dungeon and hub reachable at the route's level** is quested through (27 Oct). Known exceptions, decided: Zephras Isle (Skyborne start; skip), City of Dalaran dungeon (30-40 guide).
2. **Every Alliance dungeon in the bracket, with its quests**, as **required** chapters/steps, run once when the route reaches them, each quest labelled **normal / grind** up front (18 Oct; the label rule dates from Deviate Hides in the Era project). **Dungeons are not optional** (11 Nov 2026: Braedyn runs every one with all its quests; the 18 Oct wording said "marked optional", and the 30-60 route plan left the dungeon quests out of its XP count because of it). Only the grind/luck quests inside a dungeon are optional. Group quests outside dungeons are marked `|cFFFF0000GROUP n+|r`, optional. **Rule 3 overrides this one**: a dungeon quest whose item is a low or unknown drop is still picked up (it costs nothing on the way in) but is labelled **grind/luck**, done once, and **the dungeon is never re-run for it** — if the item doesn't drop on the run, the step says to abandon the quest. No "farm the first room for X" steps. (Precedents: Mortality Wanes in RFK at ~10%, the Baron's head, Charlga's note.)
3. **No rare-drop farms** (27 Oct). A required item drop is dropped from the guide (or made `[O]`) when it needs more than ~2× the kills the route already does there, or more than ~25 kills on fewer than ~20 spawns. Classic collections in the 20-30-kill range stay but carry a kill budget on the kill line ("~36% drop - budget ~15"). Forever items with no known rate get a budget too. Boss drops that look low on Wowhead get a "one kill only, then abandon" note. Inside dungeons this means one run, never a re-run (see rule 2).
4. **Skipped quests are silent** (27 Oct). No "don't take X" text; a quest that isn't in the guide simply has no tags. Reasons live in the audit docs, not the Lua.
5. **Cooking + Fishing are part of the route** (27 Oct). The food thread is Roasted Boar Meat → **Longjaw Mud Snapper (+10 AP, 5+)** → **Mithril Head Trout (+20 AP, 25+)**; every other recipe is skill-up filler, taken when free. Recipe vendors, trainers (Journeyman/Expert) and "cook to N" / "fish to N" stops are placed so he has the skill and level **before** he reaches the recipe. Skill targets: Cooking 50 by 12, 80 by 16, 125 by 24, 175 by ~30; Fishing 25 by 12, 50 by 15, 100 by 22, 130+ by 28. Fishing stops are `[O]`, recipe/trainer gates are plain steps. Carry this thread into every later bracket (both will be levelled for endgame anyway).
6. **Full gear guidance** for 2H Enhancement at every reward choice and dungeon (24 Sep): weapon dps > Str > Int > Spirit > Agi > spell damage; leather or cloth by score until mail at 40 (cloth Int pieces count) (3 Nov: Int 1.55 after Mental Dexterity went to 150% of Int) (13 Nov: Int 1.15, Mental Dexterity is 100% of Int again since the 12 Nov class tuning; the order per point is now weapon dps > Spirit > Str = spell damage > Int > Agi, Spirit 2.5 and spell damage 2.0 since 8 Nov). Stat weights: `docs/forever-enh-stat-weights.md`. Shaman gear lines are `[A Shaman]`.
7. **Professions:** Skinning + Mining, plus the secondaries First Aid + Cooking + Fishing; Leatherworking decision at 25 by Pristine Leather count (27 Oct), or earlier the moment the Totemic Leather Armor recipe (no Pristine Leather) is available: on 2 Nov no pattern item for it or the Pants exists on Wowhead Forever and foreverdiff lists the Armor as trainer-taught (unverified), so the trigger is the Leatherworking trainer's list, not an AH search (1 Nov rule, revised 2 Nov; docs/audit-2026-11-02.md). Camping 101 quests for all five. Journeyman/Expert steps for all of them at the Ironforge passes (Expert Fishing is Booty Bay — 30-40 guide).
8. **Shaman class quests** (Call of Earth / Fire / Water / Air) are threaded through the chapters at the right levels; trainer stops at every band (shaman trainers exist only in Dun Morogh and Ironforge).
9. **Every quest picked up is turned in or explicitly `[QS]`-abandoned**, or not picked up at all (10 Sep, Era project). `check_guide.py` enforces it.
10. **Launch-crowd layer** (25 Oct): at camped named mobs/objects, invite the people there; 2-minute rule then move on; catch-up lines say where the route returns.
11. **Optional-step test** (8 Nov, in force from chapter 27). An `[O]` quest or step stays only for a scored gear upgrade (score_items on the dated tooltips), a bag, a food-thread recipe, or a gate (class quest, trainer, profession rank, mount, reputation the route needs). XP-only optionals are out. Braedyn can keep one by name (11 Nov: A Fur Trade, because it opens Friz Frazzlespark's Powderfuse chain; the Field Marshal's Log as a conditional "if it drops" line).
12. **Drop-quest rule** (extends rule 3; thresholds confirmed 11 Nov). Kill-count quests are preferred. A mob-drop collection quest is a plain step only when its Wowhead Forever rate is 60% or better, or the item also comes from a ground object, or it is a single named mob's drop at 60%+. At 30-60% it is `[O]` with "drop quest, ~N kills" on the kill line (N = count / rate) - **unless the route's kill quests on the same mobs already cover most of that budget, when it is plain with the budget label** (Bad Medicine, Smuggler's Paradise, No Soldier Left Behind's keys). Under 30% it is out, silently, with every quest behind it in its chain. A named mob's drop between 30% and 60% gets "one kill only, then abandon". Class-quest items (the Twisted Totems) ignore the rate. Read the per-mob rows of `data/drop_rates.json`, not `overall_pct`, when the route kills only the good droppers.
13. **Elites outside dungeons are always `|cFFFF0000GROUP n+|r` and optional** (11 Nov), whatever their level, and get the optional-step test: kept only for a scored upgrade, otherwise out with every quest that needs them. Rank comes from QuestieDB npc row [5] and from the Wowhead NPC page (QuestieDB leaves many Forever ranks blank). A dungeon quest whose elites stand outside the instance door is part of the run (A Host of Evil). A class-chain gate stays a required step with the GROUP label (the Heed My Call ritual).
14. **Every chapter from 27 on opens with a level-check line** (11 Nov): "this chapter wants level N" (the highest required level among its pickups, or the level its mobs need) and what to do when under it. The XP budget of a plan counts dungeon quests as required and is computed from the same list the plan text prints.

## Writing rules — how the Lua is written
- Chapter titles are level ranges (`[N 11-15 Loch Modan]`), chained with `[NX]`; `[GA Dwarf]`/`[GA Alliance]` on chapters, `[A Shaman]` on class steps.
- **Every quest tag carries its title** (`[QA96390 Nip 'Em in the Bud]`) so new Forever quests display by name. **Every step has an explicit `[G x,y Zone]`**; NPC and mob steps carry `[TAR<id> Name]`.
- **Coordinates are Forever map space.** Stormwind and Redridge differ from Classic (`tooling\coord_transform_forever.json`: SW x' = 0.77367x + 19.68, y' = 0.77383y + 24.43; Redridge x' = x − 5.09). Forever DB spawns are already correct; Classic Wowhead numbers are not.
- `[O]` = optional step; `[OC]` = note that completes with the next step and never holds the cursor. Notes, gear calls and labels go in `[OC]`; coordinates go on quest lines, not on standalone text lines.
- **Chapter step counts matter**: Guidelime stores manual ticks by step index per chapter; when the count changes it re-matches them by exact step text and drops only the ticks of edited or removed lines (25 Sep belief corrected 2 Nov from CurrentGuide.lua:27-50). Mid-chapter edits still change the index of every later line, so edit deliberately. (Moot until he starts playing; after launch, edit chapters he has finished or not started.)
- ASCII only in the Lua (WoW fonts drop arrows and the like); LF line endings; TOC version = date of the last edit, with a one-line changelog in `## Notes`.
- Kill lines for collections: "Kill [TAR...] for N <item> [QC...] (~rate - budget ~N kills)".
- Don't invent Guidelime tags. `[CI itemId,count]` and `[UI itemId]` are in use but unverified on the Forever build (18 Oct).

## Process rules — how to edit
- **Stage the current files from the PC first**; another session may have edited them (27 Oct had two sessions editing at once). Commit with mtime guards; rebuild the zip after.
- **Verify every NPC, vendor, coordinate and skill requirement against the data**, never from a subagent's prose: `tooling\qdb\{quest,npc,object,item}.json` (QuestieDB Forever; item row 14 = vendors, row 2 = npc drops, row 10 = req level; npc row 7 = spawns by zone; no XP field), `data\zone_*.json` (Wowhead XP), `nether.wowhead.com/forever/tooltip/item/<id>` (works from a shell; carries recipe skill, food buff, drop chance), classicdb.ch for Classic drop rates (decimals are sampled; 80/100/66/50 are emulator defaults).
- **Run `python tooling\check_guide.py` after every edit** (expected: 0 flow problems, the 7 cosmetic "optional step without [G]" findings). Run `tarcheck.py` after coordinate changes.
- Large edits go to an Opus subagent ("don't write it yourself", 25 Oct); audits are read-and-report first, then Braedyn approves, then edit. Record what changed in a dated `docs/audit-<date>.md` or build doc, and bump the TOC notes.
- **Every pushed guide change ships as a GitHub release** (3 Nov): tag `v<TOC version>` on main, all three zips attached, body = install section + changelog since the previous tag. The TOC version is the date of the last edit, so a release lags the TOC the moment the zip is rebuilt and nothing is cut; check `gh release list` against the TOC versions before closing a round.
- Mark anything taken from Classic knowledge rather than Forever data as unverified, and keep an in-game test list in the build/audit doc.
