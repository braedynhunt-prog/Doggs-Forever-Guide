## Established facts
### Skyborne druid
- Exists: `SkillRaceClassInfo.csv` druid rows carry the Skyborne bits (Feral Combat 134 all races; druid Daggers/Maces/2H Maces rows Skyborne-only, Availability 1); Wowhead rows use race 95 (Alliance Skyborne) / race mask 2^32. Guidelime lists Druid under Skyborne.
- Racials (V/U mix, from the warrior research): Walk on Air (glide 10 s), Read Ley Line (+100% health/mana regen), Wind Blessed (+1% haste), Elemental Insight (+5% vs Elementals). "Skyborne Druids have new forms designed for the race" (Blizzard via maxroll, U).
- Starting gear and weapon skills: **not found anywhere** (test list).

### Feral mechanics on Forever
- **Form auto-attack DPS = equipped weapon DPS** (Blizzard deep dive, V); attack speed fixed per form (1.0 cat / 2.5 bear per a search snippet, U); weapon enchants/procs work in forms; Rake and Swipe scale with AP. So weapon dps is a first-order stat for a feral druid (unlike Classic Era).
- Cat Form (768, V): level 20, "increasing Attack Power by 12 plus Agility". Bear Form (5487, V): level 10, +120 AP, 180% armor from items, +180 health. Str-to-AP conversion in forms: not found (Classic: 2 AP per Str) - unverified.
- Feral Charge (1238122) and Primal Bite (407995) are **talents** (Blizzard deep dive, V; Wowhead tooltips show 20 / 40), not trained spells. The plan's "trained at 20 / 25" came from `SpellLevels.csv`, which lists a level for talent spells too; see the lesson of 2 Nov.
- Revive at 12 (V). Teleport: Moonglade (18960, V): 120 mana, 10 s cast, level 10, no cooldown shown.
- Trainer ranks (SkillLineAbility + SpellLevels, trainer-taught only): 4 Moonfire/Rejuvenation; 6 Thorns; 8 Entangling Roots; 10 Bear Form*/Maul/Growl/Demoralizing Roar/Teleport: Moonglade*; 12 Enrage/Regrowth/Revive; 14 Bash/Cure Poison*; 16 Aquatic Form*/Swipe; 18 Faerie Fire/Hibernate; 20 Cat Form*/Claw/Shred/Rip/Prowl/Starfire/Rebirth; 22 Soothe Animal; 24 Rake/Tiger's Fury/**Ferocious Bite (1328618, build 70753)**; 25 Lacerate (1328617, 70753); 26 Dash/Abolish Poison; 28 Claw r2/Rip r2/Cower/Challenging Roar; 30 Travel Form/Shred r2; 32 Ferocious Bite r2 (22568). (*) = on Forever these come from quests for a Skyborne (below); whether the trainer also sells them is a test item.
- Levelling consensus (U, beta-era): bear 10-19 (Swipe on 2+, Maul), cat from 20; weapon dps > Str > Agi > Sta; Ferocity -> Feral Swiftness/Instinct -> Feral Charge (20) -> Savage Fury, Sharpened Claws, Predatory Strikes -> Leader of the Pack (30).
