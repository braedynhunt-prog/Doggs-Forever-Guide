Dogg – Forever Shaman

Dwarf Shaman, 2H Enhancement, Alliance · levelling sheet 1–60 · companion to the Guidelime guide Forever Shaman 1-30
  • Weapon first. Weapon DPS is the stat: 1 DPS = 14 attack power. Rockbiter until 30, Windfury from 30 (60-min imbues, re-apply after every death).
  • Mana is the limiter. Shock once on the pull until Shamanistic Focus (20); drink, don't wait. Int = attack power from level 15 (Mental Dexterity).
  • Shaman trainers are Dun Morogh + Ironforge only. Nothing in Stormwind, Darnassus, Menethil or Darkshire. Take the tram.

At 60: 32 / 8 / 11

Enhancement 32, Elemental 8, Restoration 11. Respec and move the 19 off-tree points into Toughness / Anticipation / Improved Lightning Shield, Guardian Totems or back into Elemental for raiding once the raid build settles (raids open 9 Dec).

Why this order. Tier 2 needs 5 points in the tree, so Thundering Strikes has to come before Mental Dexterity (our Sept note said MD first; the gate makes that impossible). Stormstrike needs 15 points, so one Anticipation point at 24 is the price of Stormstrike at 25. Everything from 26 on is about mana: Improved Stormstrike, then the Resto detour for Mindfulness and Water Shield. Maelstrom and Rage are late on purpose: they are big investments that pay little before 50.

Optional respec at 25 (Icy Veins). If mana is miserable 10–24 you can spend the first 14 points in Resto (Tidal Focus, Mindfulness, Water Shield at 10 points) and respec into the Enhancement line at 25 when Stormstrike opens. Respecs are cheap early (10 s, then 1 g, rising). I would only do it if drinking is really the bottleneck in practice.

Legacy. The account-wide Legacy tree unlocks at your first level-25 character; its Talented perk starts talent points at 9–5 for later characters. Nothing changes for this first run.

Restoration picks (11)

  • Tidal Focus 5/5 (33–37): +5% hit, heals cheaper
  • Mindfulness 3/3 (38–40): 50% of regen while casting
  • Improved Healing Wave 2/5 (41–42): filler
  • Water Shield (43): mana back when hit or on a crit – use it instead of Lightning Shield

Elemental picks (8)

  • Convection 5/5 (53–57): shocks and bolts −10% mana
  • Elemental Devastation 3/3 (58–60): a spell crit gives +9% melee crit for 10 s

Mana notes

  • Healing Wave ~1.8 HP per mana; Lesser Healing Wave is for in-combat only.
  • Mental Dexterity (15) and Mental Quickness (45) turn every Int item into attack power and spell damage: "of the Eagle" is not a caster piece for you.
  • Spirit regen runs outside the 5-second rule; Improved Stormstrike (31) and Mindfulness (38) keep part of it running while casting.
  • Shamanistic Focus (20) is the point where shocking on cooldown stops costing drink time.
  • Water Shield (43): 2% of max mana per globe – swap it in for Lightning Shield the day you take it.

Changed from Classic that bites a shaman

  • Imbues last 60 min, totems 5 min, 30 yd. No Fire Nova Totem; Fire Nova is a spell on a 10 s CD.
  • Earth Shock is also a ranged interrupt and a high-threat pull.
  • Totems are raid-wide: you will be wanted in raids for the totems, not just the damage.
  • Ghost Wolf has no CD; Improved Ghost Wolf makes it instant and indoor.
  • Lightning Bolt is weak and slow (2.5 s) until Maelstrom: a pull tool, not a damage spell.
  • Windfury Weapon on your own main hand cancels your benefit from Windfury Totem; Flametongue Totem still works with it.
  • No Tranquil Air Totem. Reincarnation is a trained passive at 30.
