# Warrior variant: Phase 3 overlay spec (written 1 Nov 2026)

What the overlay for chapters 02-26 of `guide/Guidelime_ForeverWarrior` must do, with the data behind each item. Builder and syntax: `tooling/build_variant.py` (docstring) and `tooling/variants/skyborne_warrior/README.txt`. Plan: `docs/warrior-build-plan-2026-11-01.md`. The Alliance chapters are never edited; every change is an overlay file `tooling/variants/skyborne_warrior/overlay/<key>.txt`.

## How a block agent works
1. Read `docs/RULES.md`, the plan doc's "Decisions taken" and "Execution log", this file, the builder docstring, and the variant README.
2. Emit is already done: `tooling/variants/skyborne_warrior/generated_gear.json` (prot picks for every `[QT]` with reward choices, and dungeon loot groups). Do not re-run `--emit`.
3. Build to scratch: `python tooling/build_variant.py tooling/variants/skyborne_warrior/variant.json --out <scratch>/build`; read `<scratch>/build_build_report.txt`: the sections "Dropped lines", "Stripped", "Non-gear rewards", "Inline gear text" and "Needs overlay" for your chapters are the work list.
4. For each of your chapters read the Alliance source in full, then the built output, and write the overlay. Rebuild, re-read the built chapter end to end, repeat until it reads as a warrior guide.
5. Checks on the build folder with `FOREVER_GUIDE` set to it: `check_guide.py` (0 flow problems except the documented cross-block carries), `tarcheck.py`, `verify.py --all` (compare with the Alliance baseline findings: nothing new in your chapters).

## Global rules (every chapter)
- **Dropped race pairs.** The builder drops every `[A Dwarf]` / `[A Human]` line. Where the two lines were the same route step for both races (same NPC, same quests apart from the race-only ones), the step must come back as ONE untagged line via `@replace <anchor>` on the Dwarf line (an overlay line is never dropped); race-only quests inside it (Honor Students 6387/6391, Stout to Kadrell 414, Etched/Archaic Rune 3108/98581, hunter pets) are left out. The build report's "Dropped lines" list is the checklist; if a dropped line is a pure note, decide whether a warrior needs it.
- **Tags.** Route lines are written untagged (the whole addon is one class and race). Only the generated gear/loot lines carry `[A Warrior]`. `[GA Alliance]` stays on chapters. No new Guidelime tags.
- **Quest flow.** Every `[QA]` has a `[QT]` or `[QS]` in the warrior build (`check_guide.py` on the build folder). A quest the Alliance route picked up on a dropped line and completed on an untagged line later (or the reverse) must be made consistent.
- **Gear.** Generated gear lines are inserted after the `[QT]` automatically; do not write gear lines by hand for quests with choices. The nine "non-gear" quests (bag / potion / food rewards: 04 and 09 QT531, 05 QT92109 and QT103, 07 QT95065 and QT97237, 08 QT95189, 17 QT58, 22 QT95809) get an `@after QT<id>` note in the Alliance wording where it matters (bags: Hunting Quiver is useless to a warrior, take the Ammo Sack or vendor; the Night Watch Gunnysack is the bag; stews and milk as the Alliance text says). The 18 "inline gear text" step lines are reworded for a warrior (`@replace`), keeping the step.
- **Dungeon loot.** The Alliance `[OC]Loot:` lines are stripped. Put `@gear-dungeon <anchor> => <boss name>` after the boss's own step line for each boss the Alliance chapter had a Loot line for, never the bare form (trash groups carry 2-6% drops, RULES 3). The chapter's generated groups are in `generated_gear.json` "dungeons" (dropper names).
- **Flag words** (shaman, totem, Totemic, Brawler, Leatherworking, leather, hunter, pet, Rockbiter, Flametongue, Ghost Wolf, 2H, Int, Spirit): every kept line in the report's "Needs overlay" list is read; lines about the other classes are reworded or deleted; gear-stat prose becomes warrior stats (weapon dps > Str > Sta > Agi > Armor; Defense low); "leather" lines about armour type become "mail or leather, whichever scores higher"; Pristine Leather / Leatherworking decision text goes (see 14 below).
- **Professions.** Skinning + Mining, First Aid + Cooking + Fishing. All profession lines that are untagged stay; the Leatherworking-decision lines are replaced (chapter 14:9) by one `[OC]` saying the warrior keeps Skinning + Mining to 30 and that the Guard's mail set (Blacksmithing, BoP; ~34 Shadowgem and ~22 Pristine Leather for the set) is not worth switching for - check the AH for a cheap Guard's piece only out of curiosity. Camping 101: the Dun Morogh ids (96056 Skinning, 96031 Leatherworking, 96046 Mining, 96629 Cooking, 96608) are the Dwarf route's; the warrior did the Zephras versions on the island (97971 Skinning, 97970 Mining, 96646 Cooking, 97965 First Aid, 97967 Fishing) - see "Carry-overs" for any left open.
- **Warrior trainer stops** replace every shaman trainer line (dropped) and every untagged "no shaman trainer" note: warriors train in Stormwind (Wu Shen 5479 / Ilsa Corbin 5480 / Ander Germaine 914, Command Center, Old Town 80.5,59.9 Stormwind City), Ironforge (Kelv Sternhammer 5113 70.3,90.7 / Kelstrum Stonebreaker 1901 / Bilban Tosslespanner 5114, Military Ward) and Darnassus (Arias'ta Bladesinger 4087 58.7,34.9 / Darnath Bladesinger 7315 / Sildanair 4089, Warrior's Terrace). Wording as the Alliance shaman lines (`[T] train (level N: ...)`): Stormwind passes in 05 (17), 07 (19-20), 10 (22), 15 (26), 18 (28), 25 (30); Ironforge in 02 (12-14, via the flight: see 02), 03/04 (15-16), 24 (30); Darnassus in 11 (23-24) and 20 (29-30). Levels with new warrior ranks (Classic, unverified for Forever): every even level. Do not invent spell names; say "train your level N abilities".
- **Dungeon tactic lines.** Totem / shaman tactic notes become tank notes: Defensive Stance, Taunt, Shield Block on bosses, Thunder Clap for packs, pull order; keep the Alliance GROUP labels and the normal / grind labels.
- **Headers.** `@header` for every chapter whose `[D]` description mentions a shaman, totem, Call of X, hunter or the Honor Students ride (write a warrior one-liner; otherwise leave it).
- Every overlay line: ASCII, the Alliance style, `[G x,y Zone]` on step lines (DB spawn of the first `[TAR]`), quest tags with titles, launch-crowd and catch-up lines untouched.
