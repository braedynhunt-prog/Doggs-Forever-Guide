# 40-50 route plan (round 2 of the 30-60 guide, levels 42-50) - 12 Nov 2026, revised twice on 13 Nov

It fixes the chapter list and the per-quest decisions for round 2, the dungeon placement, the profession, training and gear threads, and what the specs still have to settle. It replaces the seven-line zone outline for round 2 in `docs/30-60-plan-2026-11-11.md`. Session plan: `~/.claude/plans/scalable-sauteeing-falcon.md` (approved 12 Nov). Data record: `docs/audit-2026-11-12.md`, section (4).

**In short:** 16 chapters, files `42_...lua` to `57_...lua`, in the group "Forever Shaman 30-60". 146 required quests, 499,160 quest XP, 35g 53s of quest money; another 215,400 XP is listed as conditional, grind / luck or gate. At the 65% quest share the required quests take him from 42.0 to **48.6**; with required levels enforced it is **48.0**, because eleven Un'Goro quests (51,700 XP) need level 48-50 and stay closed until he gets there (48.8 at a 60% share, with five closed). 50 needs 627,380 quest XP at this share. The round is honest about that: it ends in the Un'Goro Crater at 48-49 on required quests, and the conditional quests and three dungeon runs' kill XP decide how much of the last level is reached; what stays closed in Un'Goro opens round 3. The chapter order changed in this revision; Braedyn approved it on 13 Nov (answer 15). Order: Riverglades pickups and Krol'dok; Stormwind, the Temple door, Aerie Peak and Booty Bay; Kalimdor (Tanaris, Feralas); back east (the Hinterlands, Nethergarde and the Cape); Kalimdor again (Feralas, Zul'Farrak); a third eastern trip at 45 (Tabetha, Nethergarde, the Dalaran crater, the Searing Gorge, the Explorers' League); Kalimdor to the end (Tanaris, Maraudon, Darnassus and Azshara, Un'Goro); the closing Ironforge stop.

## How the decisions were made
The pool is every quest on Wowhead Forever of quest level 39-55 that is not Horde-side, is class-free or shaman, is open to a Dwarf, needs level 50 or less, and has no `[QA]` / `[QT]` tag in chapters 00-41, plus the prerequisite chain of each: 702 quests. The rules were applied by script (`Claude outputs/40-50-plan-2026-11-12/`: `pool_r2.py`, `decide_r2.py`, `overrides_r2.tsv`, `plan_r2.py`), then every borderline case was ruled by hand in `overrides_r2.tsv` with its reason. Per quest:
- **Elite targets outside dungeons (rule 13)**: rank from QuestieDB npc row [5] for kill targets and for every dropper of a required item, and from the Wowhead item and NPC pages; the Forever and Classic databases agree on rank for every target checked, and the named targets QuestieDB leaves unranked were read off their Wowhead pages. All out.
- **Drops (rule 12)**: the Wowhead Forever rate over all droppers, or the per-mob row when the route kills only the good dropper. 60% or better plain; 30-60% optional only for an upgrade or when the route's kill quests cover it (Water Pouch Bounty); under 30% out with the chain behind it. A named mob's item at 30-60% is one kill only (Andre Firebeard). A quest that starts from a dropped item is rated on that item (the OOX beacons, the Ship Schedule: conditional). Since the second revision six fill quests are in on a kill budget of about 25 or less (answer 12); each is marked 'fill' in `overrides_r2.tsv`.
- **Dungeons (rule 2)**: required chapters. Quests are normal, normal on a boss item (answer 4), or grind / luck.
- **No giver or no side**: a quest with no giver in the DB and none on its Wowhead page, or a Forever quest whose page states no side and whose giver has no faction in the DB, is conditional: listed, not counted.
- **Prerequisites**: quest rows [11] and [12]; a quest behind an out quest is out; a prerequisite that is neither in the pool nor done (a Horde twin) cannot be met. **Wowhead's series** (`data/wowhead_quest_links.json`) is an offer gate on top: a quest right after an out, optional or conditional quest in a series is conditional. A series is not proof of a prerequisite, which is why such a quest is kept as a conditional line at all.
- **Level and place**: `tooling/route_xp.py` on `docs/40-50-route-2026-11-12.txt`, the same list this document prints (rule 14). Quests six or more levels under the chapter's entry level are scaled. The tool lists any required quest whose prerequisite is not done or required in the same or an earlier chapter, any quest whose giver, objective or ender stands in a zone the chapters do not visit in time (a quest is listed in the chapter that hands it in), and the level the route reaches when a quest can only be accepted at its required level. On this route: no prerequisite fault, no place fault, eleven Un'Goro quests closed by level.
