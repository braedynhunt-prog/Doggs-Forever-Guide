# Audit 4 Nov 2026 - auto-completing steps, gold at the buy/train stops, cave targets (chapters 02-26)

Braedyn, playing 01b on launch day, reported three things: steps complete by themselves when he walks near an NPC; early steps tell him to buy or train several things with no idea whether he has the gold; cave mobs get a waypoint on the mob, not on the cave mouth. He asked for the chapters after the one he is on (02 Loch Modan 11-15 to 26 Razorfen Kraul) to be audited for all three. Chapters 00-01 were left alone on his decision (their gaps are listed at the end for a later round).

Data: QuestieDB Forever (current), client 1.60.1.70790, Wowhead Forever zone files pulled 2 Nov (the 70790 diff shows QuestXP/QuestInfo byte-identical, so quest money is current), Guidelime source in `_forever_\Interface\AddOns\Guidelime\` (CurrentGuide.lua updateStepCompletion, Events.lua), Wowhead Forever NPC pages read in Chrome (trainer costs), nether tooltips (sell prices).

## 1. Steps that completed on arrival

Mechanism (CurrentGuide.lua:842-950): a step completes when every completable element is complete. A step whose only completable element is its `[G]` completes once the player is within 6 yards (DEFAULT_GOTO_RADIUS). `[QA]/[QC]/[QT]`, `[XP]`, `[CI]`, `[SK]`, `[LE]` hold a step; `[T]`, `[V]`, `[R]`, `[S]`, `[P]` make it manual and it completes when the trainer/merchant/bind/taxi window opens (Events.lua TRAINER_SHOW, MERCHANT_SHOW, HEARTHSTONE_BOUND, TAXIMAP_OPENED). `[OC]` lines complete with the next step.

Scan of 02-26: 97 goto-only steps; 55 are travel and should complete on arrival; 42 were actions. Fixed by category: 12 trainer lines got `[T]` (02:52/61/62/63, 03:15/16/17, 04:60, 07:12, 08:9, 10:17, 18:28); 6 vendor lines got `[V]` (14:29, 19:57, 19:94, 23:14, 23:48, 24:21) and the AH dynamite got `[CI4365,10]` (05:13); 5 bandage/cook stops got `[SK FirstAid N]` / `[SK Cooking 175]` and 5 optional fishing stops `[SK Fishing N]` (22:15 and 23:42 became `[OC]` reminders); 7 loot/use steps got the quest or item that holds them (02:132 `[QC94467]`, 11:66 bowl `[CI12347,1]`, 11:99 the three samples, 11:122 horn `[CI13536,1]`, 21:13 key `[CI5475,1]`, 22:21 `[QC943,1]`, 24:44 `[CI9280,1]`). `check_guide.py` now flags any goto-only action step ("goto-only action step (completes on arrival)").

Unverified on the Forever build: `[SK <skill> <n>]` (GuideParser.lua:683 parses it; skill names FIRSTAID/COOKING/FISHING in Data/SkillDB.lua) and `[CI]` holding a step until the count is in the bags.
