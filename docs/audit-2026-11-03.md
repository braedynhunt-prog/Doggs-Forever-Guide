What the post changes that touches this project (everything else - gamepad, PvP, other classes - does not):
- **Shaman, Enhancement:** Mental Dexterity 50/100/150% of Int (was 33/67/100%); Stormstrike 150% normalized weapon damage, 6 s cooldown (was 100%, 8 s); Maelstrom Weapon trigger chance doubled; Flurry 6/12/18/24/30% (was 5-25%); Improved Ghost Wolf moved from row 2 to row 4. Shaman general: Lightning Bolt base damage up on all ranks (max rank +80%, each rank ~78% of Classic Era base), Chain Lightning 2.5 s cast / ~145% LB damage / ~225% LB mana, all shocks reverted to Classic Era base damage, Totemic Projection renamed Totemic Call (teleports totems to the shaman, 30 s cooldown), Windfury animates an extra swing. Elemental: Lava Burst removed, Call of Thunder added, Lightning Overload 10/20/30%.
- **Druid, Feral:** Lacerate is now a Feral talent (row 4, 1 point) and not trained; Ferocious Bite gained a rank 1 at 24 (old ranks relabelled one higher); Shred 200%, Claw 140%, Ravage 435% weapon damage; Rip/Rake/Ferocious Bite/Pounce base and AP scaling +~25%; Berserk removed; Primal Bite is a row-7 1-point talent (needs Leader of the Pack); Heart of the Wild swapped with Furor (now Feral); Poised passive at 32; bear threat bonus 115%.
- **Warrior:** Defensive Stance threat 80% (was 30%); Defiance 7/13/20%; Slam cooldown 15 s; Arms Two-Handed Weapon Specialization 2/4/6%; Fury Flurry 3-point 10/20/30% without Death Wish, Enrage row 3, Bloodthirst needs Death Wish, new Reckless Swings and Improved Whirlwind.
- **Races:** the 26 Oct racial pass repeated "for completeness" (Dwarf: Earth Fury removed, Artisan's Precision +1% hit; Skyborne: Wind-Blessed 2%, Read Leyline 50% for 15 s / Attuned 1 h at 100%, Skysight 15% / Charged 30% for 1 h; Human: Ruthless 1%, Human Spirit 10%). All already in the guides (shaman 01a/sheet, druid and warrior 00s).
- **General:** training costs equalised, 206-207 g for every class 1-60; learning a spell no longer puts it on the action bar; bags no longer auto-equip; "adjusted experience values on various quests", "adjusted pre-requisites and level settings on some quests", respawn rates raised for launch (fastest in starting zones, to be lowered later).
- **Named quest fixes on the route:** Carnage (98283, Wetlands) no longer resets near the end of the fight; Alba Fairmoon stays available to turn in Destruction in Deadmines (92753/92819); Holy Diver (87760, Darkshore) now gives XP (not in any guide: needs all nine beached quests). Ruins of Lordaeron dungeon quests all need level 15 now (was a mix of 15 and 16; the guide enters at 20).
