# Review of the Forever Shaman leveling sheet - 3 Nov 2026

Scope: `docs/forever-shaman-leveling-sheet.html` / `.pdf` (Braedyn's one-screen companion, built 2 Nov 2026 from client 1.60.1.70707), checked line by line against the current data on 3 Nov 2026, the day before launch. Nothing in the sheet or the guide was edited; this doc lists what to change. Section 3 lists guide lines the check exposed as wrong; they need Braedyn's approval before an edit.

Data used (all checked 3 Nov, `check_sources.py`: questiedb OK 1.0.10, client OK 1.60.1.70755, wowhead_xp OK 27/27 of 3 Nov, item_tooltips STALE by date only since 70755 is byte-identical to 70753):
- Talents and 3 Nov hotfixes: wowforevertalents.com (/shaman/, all 50 talent pages, /abilities/shaman/, /patch-notes/, /legacy/, /roadmap/, /racials/, /professions/), fetched 3 Nov; it reads client 70755 plus the server hotfixes of 3 Nov. Blizzard's 2 Nov notes: us.forums.blizzard.com thread 2360696 post 9 (JSON), see `docs/audit-2026-11-03.md`.
- Spell levels and ranks: `tooling/client/1.60.1.70755/` SpellName, SpellLevels, SkillLineAbility, SkillRaceClassInfo (shaman skill lines 373/374/375, ClassMask 64). No shaman row differs between 70707 and 70755.
- NPCs, quests, items: `tooling/qdb/*.json` (QuestieDB Forever 1.0.10), `data/zone_*.json` (Wowhead XP, 3 Nov), `weights/tooltips/2026-11-02.json`, `weights/scores_2026-11-03.txt`, `docs/forever-enh-stat-weights.md`, `docs/RULES.md`, the guide `.lua` files.

Roughly 330 claims were checked; about 290 hold. The rest are below.

## 3. Guide lines the check exposed (proposals, not applied)

1. `08_RuinsOfLordaeron.lua:13` Call of Water "~10,500 XP" -> "~9,600 XP" (sum 9,580 from the zone data).
2. `08_RuinsOfLordaeron.lua:49` Segmented Spider Leg "a straight upgrade over Smite's Hammer or Rockslicer" is wrong on the current weights (257.7 vs 301.3 / 289.8); Corpse Chopper (254.7) likewise. Propose: "a fallback if you have neither Smite's Hammer nor Rockslicer; keep either of those over it".
3. `09_LochModanMenethil.lua:62` "Strong Pole (+45)" -> "(+5)", and the fish-to-130 maths with it (base ~40 + 5 + 50 = ~95): the step should say the trout dock is a long shot at 21-22 and that 18:26 is the real trout stop, or be reworded as "fish for Longjaw, trout if your skill is near 130".
4. `02_LochModan.lua` has no shaman Two-Handed Axes step although 02:150 equips Mountaineer's Greataxe and later chapters take Rockslicer, Reef Axe, Corpsemaker and Thermaplugg's Left Arm. Propose a `[A Shaman]` step at Buliwyf Stonehand `[TAR11865]` `[G61.2,89.5 Ironforge]` on the chapter-02 Ironforge pass: "train Two-Handed Axes (10 silver) - the client lists 2H Maces as baseline for shamans and 2H Axes as weapon-master taught (unverified until seen in game)".
5. Eldrun Stormbreaker's coordinate: 46.2,13.2 (02/03/10/11) vs 46.6,13.4 (15/18/22/24/25) vs DB 47.1,13.8. Pick one (the DB spawn, or whichever is right in game) and use it in all nine lines; `tarcheck.py` after.
6. Bruegs Kindleborn 87.6,43.6 (01b:197, 02:133) vs DB 87.94,44.8: 1.2 off in y, within the DB's usual noise, check in game.
7. `02_LochModan.lua:48` "Ironforge and Kharanos are the ONLY shaman trainers on this route": true for the route, but the DB also has a Hinterlands trainer; wording only.
8. Tooling, not the guide: `weights/score_items.py` still reads `weights/item_tooltips.json` (the 24 Sep scrape), whose Rockslicer row says +10 Str (tooltip of 2 Nov: +7 Str +7 Nature Resistance) and Ironforge Greathammer +4 Str +7 Sta (tooltip: no stats, 42 Fire proc). So `scores_2026-11-03.txt` lines 210 and 216 are off (Rockslicer 295.8 should be 289.8). Any weapon comparison should be re-run from `tooltips/2026-11-02.json` before it goes into a guide line.

## 4. Unverified (not checkable from the data; in-game test list)

- Totemic Recall's 25% refund: tooltip says so, but an earlier patch-notes entry says the refund spell's amount reads 0 in the tables. Also the hotfix "Totemic Recall costs 22 silver (was 70)".
- Windfury and Flametongue Totems are mutually exclusive on a target (patch-notes hotfix); Grace of Air no longer exclusive with either. The sheet's totem table puts Windfury Totem and Flametongue Totem in the same group set.
- Two-Handed Maces baseline vs Two-Handed Axes trainer-taught: SkillLineAbility AcquireMethod 1 vs 2, both SkillRaceClassInfo Availability 0; the codes are undocumented, so this stays an inference. Dual Wield row is AcquireMethod 3 (rune-style), Mail skill 413 MinLevel 40 (supports "mail at 40").
- Skinning +5% damage to Beasts / Mining +5% health: no source on the talent site's /professions/ page or the Wowhead spell tooltips; only our own README and audit-2026-11-01 (which say Beasts and Dragonkin).
- "Searing Totem attacks the nearest enemy" (tooltip: "an enemy within 20 yards"), "Fire Nova threat goes to you", "First Aid makes the healing potions" (README-stated only), Ankh as the Reincarnation reagent (Wowhead spell 20608 says Reagents: Ankh, 1 h cooldown; the guide never mentions an Ankh), "Grounding/Windfury/Grace of Air need Call of Air" (guide 25:7 says only "the Air Totem"), dual spec at 40, Apprentice Riding at 40.
- The 10-19 shock policy (85-mana shock vs 1.2 s of melee) rests on the beta shock damage in `enh_weights.py` EARTH_SHOCK (18/33/49/81); Blizzard reverted shocks to Classic Era base damage on 3 Nov. Revisit with in-game shock tooltips.

Resolved from the 3 Nov notes (drop from the sheet's "Check in game" list): the racial is Artisan's Precision (+1% hit), Earth Fury is gone from the racials page; training costs are equalised at 206-207 g per class for 1-60 (Blizzard), so "trainer prices not in the client data" has a published total.
