Scope: `docs/forever-shaman-leveling-sheet.html` / `.pdf` (Braedyn's one-screen companion, built 2 Nov 2026 from client 1.60.1.70707), checked line by line against the current data on 3 Nov 2026, the day before launch. Nothing in the sheet or the guide was edited; this doc lists what to change. Section 3 lists guide lines the check exposed as wrong; they need Braedyn's approval before an edit.

Data used (all checked 3 Nov, `check_sources.py`: questiedb OK 1.0.10, client OK 1.60.1.70755, wowhead_xp OK 27/27 of 3 Nov, item_tooltips STALE by date only since 70755 is byte-identical to 70753):

Blizzard's 2 Nov notes: us.forums.blizzard.com thread 2360696 post 9 (JSON), see `docs/audit-2026-11-03.md`.

## 4. Items the data could not settle, checked on the web (3 Nov evening)

Windfury Totem and Flametongue Totem retain their exclusivity"). Windfury Weapon on your own main hand still disables Windfury Totem for you (WFT tooltip). - **Launch and raids**: Blizzard's launch article says 4 Nov 3:00 p.m. PST (one paragraph says PDT; daylight time ended 1 Nov, so PST = 23:00 UTC = 6 p.m. Toronto). Raids 9 Dec: WFT roadmap and WT roadmap (13 Sep); no Blizzard sentence read directly.
- **Camping 101: Cooking exists** (qdb 96629 at Eric Brighthammer 265813, plus copies at the other camp NPCs), so the sheet's "one per profession" and the guide's 01b:15 stand; WP's claim that Cooking has none is wrong for our DB. Camping facts from the guide sites: the campfire buff needs about 1 minute sitting (BZ posts 1 Oct / 8 Oct: sitting, chair, sleeping, lying down or kneeling all count); the Leatherworking Camp Tent gives rested XP (WP: up to 5% of a level, once an hour, a debuff blocks it until then; tier 1 at skill 20, 5 Light Leather); Mining Lodestone gives attack power, Skinning Camp Chair crit, First Aid Kit stamina (WT); Camping 101 is optional because the trainer also teaches the object (WP).
- **Respec cost**: BZ post 1 (beta): "If you have not recently changed your talents, the cost is now 1 silver to re-spec. This is temporary for the Beta only. After your first talent reset, the subsequent cost will be much higher, but it will drop to 1 silver after 1 hour has passed." BZ post 6: Legacy respec 1 s, falling by one respec per hour, beta only. WFT 3 Nov: hidden Class / Legacy Talent Reset counters, 10 each, one recharging every 7 days on live. So the live rule is a decaying counter; no live price is published. The sheet's "10 s, then 1 g, rising" is unsourced: replace with "1 silver when you have not respecced recently (beta rule; live price unpublished, counter recharges one per 7 days)".
- **Legacy**: 16 Legacy Points spendable at launch (WFT /legacy/, BZ topic 2368776 gave beta testers 16 to test); points come from challenges, three per class for reaching 25 / 45 / 60.
