## Model changes (`weights/enh_weights.py`; bands 10/15/20 must reproduce the 8 Nov numbers)
1. `Scenario` gains `imbue` ("rockbiter" | "windfury"), talents `ss` (0/1), `iss` (0-2), `flurry` (0-5), and uses `weapon_speed`: damage per swing = wdps x speed + AP x speed / 14 (same total as today's dps form; only the per-swing split is new).
2. **Windfury**: per landed swing, 20% chance of 2 extra attacks, each worth one swing plus the rank's AP (104 at 30, 221 at 40) x speed / 14; extra attacks hit, crit and miss like swings and do not proc again (wowsims uses a 1.5 s ICD; irrelevant at 2H speeds). Rockbiter stays the flat-AP path. The imbue is a switch so both are run per band.
3. **Stormstrike** (when `ss`): one extra 150% weapon strike every 6 s of fight time at 8% of `base_mana(level)`, eligible for Windfury procs; every Earth Shock while SS is talented does 1.2x (the shock policy already fires every 6 s). Improved Stormstrike: expected mana back per SS = 0.2 x 15 s x 0.5 x the regen rate, added to the pool; ignore the dodge/parry reset.
4. **Flurry**: attack-speed multiplier 1 + h(rank) x P, P = 1 - (1 - c)^3 with c the melee crit chance; h per rank from the pulled values.
5. Rank tables: EARTH_SHOCK add R5 (36) and R6 (48); LIGHTNING_SHIELD add R5 (40) and R6 (48); ROCKBITER corrected; drink/food tiers at 35 and 45.
6. `band_scenarios()`: 25 gets Stormstrike, 30 gets Stormstrike + Flurry 2/5 + Improved Stormstrike 2/2, both with the imbue switch (Rockbiter R5/R6 vs Windfury R1); **40** added with the gear set above, Windfury R2 vs Rockbiter R6, Earth Shock R5, Lightning Shield R5, and the sheet's 31 points at 40 (TS 5, MD 3, AK 2, SF, EW 3, Anticipation 1, SS, IGW 2, Flurry 2, ISS 2, Spirit Weapons, Tidal Focus 5, Mindfulness 3; the Resto points and Spirit Weapons do nothing in the model).
7. `weights/profiles.py`: `BANDS` + 40; `enh_band_weights()` takes Int and Agi from the model for 40 like the other bands (Spi 2.5 / SP 2.0 / Exp 3 stay); `score_items.usable()` gains a `level` argument so Mail is usable from 40; `tooling/gear_calls.py` and `tooling/dungeon_loot.py` pass the chapter's band to it. `nearest_band` unchanged. The 1-30 gear reports must come out identical (diff them).
8. **Talent check** (`weights/talent_check.py`): runs bands 25/30/40 with the sheet's order against at least two alternatives (Flurry 5/5 before Improved Ghost Wolf and Anticipation; Enhancement-only to 40 - Toughness / Anticipation instead of the Resto dip) and prints kills/hour per build. Recommendation goes into the doc; the sheet is Braedyn's to change.
9. **Weapon verdict**: kills/hour at 40 for Rage of the Storm vs Bonebiter under both imbues, with and without Stormstrike (Rage's +10% = 1.1 on the SS strike); the result is written into `docs/30-60-plan-2026-11-11.md` chapter 39 (replace the "Braedyn's call" sentence) and the professions line that mentions the imbue.
